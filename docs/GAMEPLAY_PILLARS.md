# Gameplay pillars

## Product identity

AstroSpace: Quantum Awakening is an exploration-first science-fiction action RPG. The player is an Awakened explorer whose body and abilities are linked to an ancient quantum lattice. The fantasy is not simply becoming the strongest fighter; it is earning the ability to reach, understand and survive extraordinary places.

## Experience priorities

These are design priorities for production reviews, not measured telemetry:
1. Exploration and discovery — primary.
2. Traversal, flight and mastery of character powers — primary.
3. Narrative, environmental storytelling and world history — primary.
4. Combat and boss encounters — supporting pillar with high craft quality.
5. Social play, collection and customization — long-term support.

A normal play session should offer meaningful non-combat activities: surveying, navigation, ruin investigation, specimen collection, rescue, environmental puzzles, anomaly research, route mapping or assisting an expedition.

## Player verbs

- Walk, climb, glide, dash, hover and use controlled free-flight powers.
- Move in six degrees of freedom while in zero gravity or space.
- Scan terrain, organisms, energy signatures, structures and cosmic phenomena.
- Record discoveries into a persistent codex and connect evidence to quest chains.
- Solve multi-step environmental puzzles and activate ancient mechanisms.
- Pilot ships, tune modules and chart safe or dangerous routes.
- Make risk/reward decisions around time-limited events and unstable worlds.
- Fight only where the world, story or player choice makes combat meaningful.

## Exploration loop

Observe → scan → form a hypothesis → find evidence → solve or traverse an obstacle → unlock a route or story layer → record the discovery → prepare for a more dangerous destination.

A discovery should change what the player knows, where they can go, or how they understand the world. Avoid empty map markers and repetitive collectathons.

## Combat philosophy

Combat is deliberate, readable and spectacular. Every player skill and boss attack needs a clear anticipation phase, a readable danger shape, an impact, a response window and a recovery. Bosses should test movement, environmental awareness and mastery of powers instead of demanding only damage output.

No progression-critical story should require repeated boss farming. At least one meaningful combat-free or low-combat approach should exist in each major quest chain.

## Progression contract

The starting planet is a protected onboarding space. The player cannot launch into open space merely by pressing a button. Exiting requires a completed story authorization, a functional ship and its quantum navigation core. Later destinations can require combinations of story milestones, player capability, equipment, scanner knowledge, environmental protection and route intelligence.

## Content quality gate

Each planet or major location must have:
- A visual identity and a distinct silhouette visible from orbit.
- At least one memorable traversal feature.
- A coherent ecological and geological logic.
- A layered history told through places, not only dialogue.
- At least one discovery chain with a meaningful reveal.
- A local soundscape and weather/atmospheric signature.
- A unique event, hazard or world-state change.
- A clear reason to revisit after the first expedition.
