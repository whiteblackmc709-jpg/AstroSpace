# Exploration, flight and destination access

## Starting-world rule

Kharon's Rest is the first and only accessible surface world during the opening tutorial. It is intentionally not an open-space launch pad. The orbital launch authorization is a narrative and gameplay milestone, not a default feature available from minute one.

The first exit requires all of:
- Completion of the launch authorization story chain (chapter/stage 3 in the reference rules).
- A ship assigned to the character.
- A working quantum navigation core.
- Completion of the flight safety and navigation tutorial.

The requirements are checked by authoritative gameplay logic. A hidden menu, alternate route or client-side flag must not bypass the gate.

## Unlock dimensions

A destination can require any combination of:
- Main-story chapter or quest flags.
- Minimum character level or ability mastery.
- Ship class, drive range, hull rating or environmental equipment.
- Scanner/codex knowledge acquired from an earlier expedition.
- Faction standing, a rescue outcome or a discovered navigation coordinate.
- A time window or a cosmic event phase.

Level alone should not unlock every destination. Story knowledge and preparation are important parts of exploration.

## Flight fantasy

Ground movement grows into controlled hover and short bursts of free flight, then zero-gravity movement and six-degree-of-freedom ship flight. Character powers should support traversal: gravity anchoring, short-range blink, momentum shaping, energy tethering, void glide or temporary shields against environmental forces.

Flight needs:
- Readable acceleration, braking, drift and orientation.
- Clear orientation/velocity cues and reorientation assistance.
- Safe landing and docking affordances.
- Motion-sickness settings and remappable controls.
- Distinct atmospheric and vacuum handling.
- Server-authoritative position and collision for multiplayer.

## Expedition design

Each destination should answer four questions before the player travels:
1. What can be discovered there?
2. What preparation is needed?
3. What can go wrong?
4. What new route, knowledge or capability can be earned?

The player should see why a world is locked and the exact missing conditions. When a gate is met, the unlock should feel like a meaningful expedition milestone.
